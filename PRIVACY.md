# Privacy Policy - TextSnap Contacts Extractor

**Effective Date:** October 2025  

TextSnap Contacts Extractor (“the Extension”) respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal information.  

## Information We Collect
The extension does **not collect, store, or share** any personally identifiable information, WhatsApp messages, or contacts.  
All contact extraction happens locally in your browser — no data leaves your device.  

We only collect **anonymous usage analytics** through [Aptabase](https://aptabase.com/), such as how often the extension is used.  
This data is aggregated and used solely to improve user experience and performance.  

## How We Use Data
Collected analytics are used only for debugging and improvement.  
No advertising, profiling, or tracking of individual users takes place.  

## Data Sharing
We do not sell or share any data with third parties.  
All data remains anonymous and compliant with privacy regulations.  

## Permissions
- **activeTab** – Allows extracting contacts from the active WhatsApp Web tab.  
- **storage** – Used to store extracted contacts and preferences locally within the browser.  

## Host Access
The extension only works on `https://web.whatsapp.com/` to extract contacts.  
It does not access or monitor any other sites.  

## Third-Party Services
We use [Aptabase](https://aptabase.com/) for anonymous analytics.  
Aptabase does not collect personal data and follows GDPR and other privacy laws.
